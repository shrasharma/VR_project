Understanding Fear of Heights (Acrophobia)

Fear of heights, or acrophobia, is one of the most common specific phobias. It involves an intense fear when a person is exposed to heights—such as tall buildings, bridges, or mountains. Even thinking about heights can trigger dizziness, anxiety, or panic.

This fear often develops from negative experiences or our brain’s overactive safety mechanism. While a little caution is natural, acrophobia can restrict daily activities and limit freedom.
