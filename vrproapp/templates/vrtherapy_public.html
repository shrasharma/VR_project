How VR Therapy Helps with Fear of Heights

VR therapy safely exposes you to virtual height scenarios — like standing on a balcony, crossing a bridge, or looking down from a skyscraper.

These realistic but controlled experiences help you gradually desensitize your fear response. You stay safe while your brain learns to manage stress and anxiety.

With continuous exposure and relaxation guidance, you build confidence to face real-world heights calmly.
