Understanding Fear of Spiders (Arachnophobia)

Arachnophobia is an extreme fear of spiders, often triggered by their appearance or sudden movements. Even harmless spiders can cause overwhelming panic or avoidance behaviors.

This fear may arise from early negative encounters, cultural conditioning, or inherited instincts. Severe arachnophobia can interfere with normal living, preventing people from entering certain places or cleaning rooms.
