How VR Therapy Helps with Fear of Spiders

VR therapy exposes you gradually to virtual spiders in lifelike environments—like a room corner or a garden.

Unlike real exposure, you remain completely safe, allowing your mind to adapt and reduce its exaggerated fear response.

With repeated VR sessions, anxiety decreases, and confidence increases, preparing you to face real-life situations calmly.
