Understanding Public Speaking Anxiety (Glossophobia)

Glossophobia is the intense fear of speaking before an audience. It’s one of the most common social fears and can cause physical symptoms like trembling, sweating, or voice quivering.

This anxiety stems from self-consciousness, fear of judgment, or previous negative experiences. But the good news is—it’s completely treatable with gradual exposure and confidence-building exercises.
